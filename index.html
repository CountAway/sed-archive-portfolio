2024.11.28 ESSAY

On the Convergence of Art and Data

The boundary between aesthetic judgment and empirical observation has never been particularly well-defined. What we call "data visualization" is, in many respects, an act of translation — the conversion of numerical relationships into spatial and chromatic ones. This essay examines the epistemological implications of that translation, arguing that the most effective visualizations are those that acknowledge their own artificiality rather than pretending to transparent representation.

2024.09.14 FIELD NOTES

Field Notes: Observing Urban Strata

Over the course of six weeks, I walked every major commercial corridor in three districts of Shanghai, photographing and cataloguing the visible layers of architectural history. What emerged was not a simple narrative of old giving way to new, but a complex palimpsest in which successive generations of construction, renovation, and demolition had created a stratified record — readable, if you knew how to look.

2024.06.03 REFLECTION

The Aesthetics of Empiricism

There is a particular beauty in the systematic — in the careful arrangement of specimens, the precise calibration of instruments, the patient accumulation of observations. This reflection considers whether the aesthetic dimension of scientific practice is merely incidental or whether it plays a constitutive role in the production of knowledge. Drawing on examples from geological survey, astronomical observation, and data visualization, I argue for the latter.
